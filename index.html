What Scavenger finds

Three levels of detection, all offline

Exact Duplicates

SHA-256 byte-level comparison. Catches renamed files, copies in different folders, and cloud sync duplicates.

Near-Duplicates

Perceptual hashing detects images and videos that look identical but differ in compression, format, or minor edits.

File Versions

Fuzzy filename matching finds report.pdf, report_v2.pdf, report_final.pdf and groups them as versions.

100% Offline

No network requests, no cloud uploads, no telemetry. Your files never leave your device.

How it works

Three steps, nothing automatic

01

Scan a folder

Point Scavenger at any folder. It indexes every file, computes hashes, and groups duplicates — all locally, all in seconds.

02

Review suggestions

Browse grouped results side-by-side. Select which copies to remove. Scavenger pre-selects the likely duplicates but you stay in control.

03

Confirm & delete

Every deletion requires an explicit confirmation. Files are only removed after you approve — no auto-deletion, ever.
